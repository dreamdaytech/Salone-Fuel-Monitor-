## Pitch by prospect type

### Business/economic newspapers

Lead with a specific data story rather than a request for a backlink:

> We have compiled Sierra Leone’s 2026 petrol and diesel changes by effective date, with a transparent methodology and source links. If useful for your fuel, transport, inflation or cost-of-living reporting, you are welcome to cite the timeline or request a clean table/chart.

### General media

Offer the source as a reporter utility:

> Salone Fuel Monitor now has dedicated current-price pages, an effective-date history and a methodology page that distinguishes official pump prices from missing or calculated values. We would be happy to provide a reporter-ready table for fuel stories.

### Business associations

Focus on operational value:

> The platform provides current pump-price context, historical changes and West Africa comparisons that may be useful to members assessing transport, distribution and operating costs.

### Data/research institutions

Focus on definitions and reproducibility:

> We maintain a public effective-date series and document how missing values, multiple changes in one month and regional currency conversion are handled. We welcome feedback and research use with attribution.

## Outreach rules

- Personalise the opening line to a recent article, report or organisational role.
- Link to one primary asset, not five unrelated pages.
- Offer a chart, table or clarification before asking for anything.
- Never buy editorial links or offer payment for a “dofollow” citation.
- One follow-up after 5–7 business days is enough.
- Track responses, citations and the URL that earned the link.
- Where a media house has already written about fuel, pitch a complementary dataset or correction resource rather than a duplicate article.
