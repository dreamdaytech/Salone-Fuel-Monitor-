## Editorial Rules for Fuel Content

- Use the NPRA as the primary source for Sierra Leone regulated pump prices and pricing methodology.
- Always state the effective date when publishing a fuel price.
- Never infer a missing kerosene/petrol/diesel value from another fuel product.
- Treat blank, null and zero as no confirmed price for that fuel event unless the source explicitly reports zero as a real value.
- Do not collapse multiple price changes in the same month into one record.
- For regional comparison, normalize to a common unit and record the comparison date.
- Distinguish official national pump prices from brand-level, city-level or third-party benchmark prices.
- Avoid claims such as “cheapest in West Africa” unless the comparison set, date and data source are explicitly stated.
- Keep dated comparison articles updated or clearly label them as historical snapshots.

## Monthly NPRA Fuel-Price Update Template

### Suggested title

`Sierra Leone Fuel Price Update — [Month YYYY]: Petrol, Diesel & Kerosene`

### Suggested SEO title

`Sierra Leone Fuel Prices [Month YYYY] | Petrol, Diesel & Kerosene`

### Suggested structure

1. Short answer: current official prices and effective date.
2. Table with petrol, diesel, kerosene and fuel oil if officially available.
3. Change from previous effective-date record for each fuel independently.
4. What changed and what did not change.
5. NPRA explanation or pricing context, if published.
6. Global oil / exchange-rate context without implying direct one-to-one causation.
7. Transport/business implications stated carefully as likely effects, not guaranteed outcomes.
8. Internal links to current price pages, Price Trends, Regional Comparison and Data Methodology.
9. Primary-source link to NPRA.

### Publication rule

Do not create a new article merely because the calendar month changed. Publish when there is a meaningful official price update, policy announcement, subsidy change, supply event or market development that gives the page unique value.

## Backlink Strategy

### Tier 1 — Official and institutional citations

Targets:

- Government or regulator resource pages where an independent public fuel-data resource is relevant.
- Universities and research institutions working on economics, energy, transport, public policy or data.
- Development organisations publishing Sierra Leone economic or cost-of-living resources.
- Business associations, logistics groups and transport-sector organisations.

Pitch angle: Salone Fuel Monitor provides a public, date-based fuel-price history and regional comparison resource that can be cited in research, public information and market analysis.

### Tier 2 — Sierra Leone media

Targets should include credible national newspapers, radio/TV digital desks, business publications and data-focused online outlets.

Pitch angles:

- “A clean historical dataset showing how petrol and diesel changed in 2026.”
- “A same-date comparison of Sierra Leone vs neighbouring West African fuel markets.”
- “Explainer: why falling crude oil does not always immediately reduce Sierra Leone pump prices.”
- “Data source for reporters covering transport fares, inflation, logistics and cost of living.”

Do not ask journalists for a backlink as the first sentence. Offer a useful chart, table, historical record or methodology page they can cite.

### Tier 3 — Regional and international data publishers

Targets:

- West Africa economic newsletters and business publications.
- Energy and commodity data blogs.
- Transport/logistics publications.
- African data journalism projects.
- Country research pages that currently cite generic global datasets but lack a Sierra Leone local source.

Pitch angle: Salone Fuel Monitor supplies local Sierra Leone context that a regional aggregator may not capture, including effective-date records, methodology and transport-market context.

## Anchor-Text Policy

Avoid repeatedly requesting the exact same keyword anchor. A natural backlink profile should include:

- Salone Fuel Monitor
- Sierra Leone fuel price data
- Sierra Leone fuel price history
- fuel prices in Sierra Leone
- West Africa fuel price comparison
- this fuel-price dataset
- this methodology
- bare URL citations

Do not pay for low-quality directory links or mass-submit exact-match anchors. Relevance and editorial context are more valuable than volume.
